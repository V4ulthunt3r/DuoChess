The case

The phone is the case. The display is the board.

No start screen, no menus to wade through. Duo-Chess opens as a closed chess case in the finish you chose. Unfold the iPhone Duo and you look straight at the board.

  • Lift the clasp, then unfold

    The clasp lifts around its pin with a soft mechanical tap. Released, the case waits for you to open the phone — the real hinge does the rest.

  • The fold runs between the players

    Ranks follow the long side, so you sit at opposite ends. The two halves of the board meet exactly at the fold, between the fourth and fifth rank.

  • Fold it shut, mid-game

    Closing the phone locks the lid again and hides every control. The position, the clocks and even an unfinished promotion are waiting when you open it.

In action

A whole game in thirty seconds.

Scholar’s mate on Classic Walnut with smooth piece moves switched on: legal squares appear as studs, the knight leaps, the queen strikes on f7 — and the winning king takes the podium.

Details

Pieces with weight.

Smooth piece moves

Optional: knights leap in an arc, bishops lean, pawns step and rooks glide close to the board. Every finish tunes its own motion. Off by default.

Wood on wood

A dry placement sound, a lower strike for captures, paired taps for castling — with matching haptics. Sound and haptics have separate switches and follow the silent switch.

A podium for the winner

At the end, the winning king rises between its queen and rook in your finish’s own pieces. A draw has both kings bow. The final position stays one tap away.

Piece size

Scale the chessmen from 70% to 120% with a live preview. Squares and touch targets keep their size.

Game Center

Twelve achievements, from your first castle to all four lessons solved without hints, and leaderboards for solo wins at every strength. Optional — guests play just the same.

For everyone

VoiceOver names every square and announces legal moves. Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast are all respected.

Privacy

Just you, your opponent and the board.

Duo-Chess has no account, no analytics and no server of its own, and it launches without ads. Your games and settings stay on the device.

Saved on the device

The position, clocks, training progress and your preferences are stored locally. Deleting the app removes them.

No permissions needed

No camera, no contacts, no location. Duo-Chess doesn’t ask for any access.

Apple services only

Purchases go through the App Store, and Game Center is optional. I receive no payment or account data.

No tracking at all

No third-party SDKs, nothing shared for advertising. Details in the privacy policy.
