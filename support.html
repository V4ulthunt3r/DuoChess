Help with Duo-Chess on iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad

Write to me

Something not working, or an idea for the next finish? Email info@hildel.com. Please include your device model, the iOS version and the finish you were using — that helps me reproduce it quickly.

The case

How do I open the case on iPhone Duo?

Tap the clasp on the right edge of the lid to release it, then unfold the phone. The board appears across both halves of the inner display. Fold the phone shut and the locked lid returns; your game is kept.

And on a regular iPhone or iPad?

There is no hinge to follow, so the lid opens virtually: tap the clasp and it swings open. Use the brass menu fitting → Close box to close it again.

What happens when I stand the Duo half open?

The board moves to the lower half and the upper half becomes a desk: the free chess stage, or with Duo-Chess Pro the training station, analysis desk or chess clock. Unfold fully and the full board is back. On other devices you can switch under Board menu → Tabletop.

I closed the phone during a clock duel. Did the clock stop?

No — like a real chess clock, time keeps running while the case is closed or the app is in the background. Use the brass menu to pause and resume both clocks.

Playing

How do I start a different mode?

Open the brass menu fitting and choose New game. Pick Two players, Solo practice, Clock duel, Analysis board or Training, then the options for that mode.

Why did the game end in a draw?

Two-player games are drawn automatically after the same position occurs three times, or after fifty moves by each side without a pawn move or capture. The analysis board keeps such lines playable.

Can I take a move back?

Yes, use Undo in the brass menu. On the analysis board Redo replays the line again, until you play a different move.

The pieces feel too small or too large.

Game options → Piece size scales the chessmen from 70% to 120%. Use default size returns to 100%. Squares and touch targets keep their size.

How does Game Center work?

Board menu → Game Center lets you sign in and shows your progress. Sign in before starting a game so it counts. Only solo games from the initial position without undo count towards the leaderboards. As a guest, everything stays on the device.

There’s no sound.

Placement sounds follow the silent switch and the volume, just like other ambient sounds. Sound and haptics each have their own switch in Game options.

Finishes and purchases

What is free, and what costs extra?

Unlimited chess for two and selected base finishes are free, without ads at launch. Duo-Chess Pro ($9.99, one-time) adds the Crystal Ice, Obsidian Gold and Pure Glass finishes, the extended desks for the half-open Duo and the complete chess trainer. Ten collector sets cost $1.99 each, or come in three bundles: vehicles and holidays for $5.99 each, knights and Vikings for $2.99. Prices are US launch prices and may differ in your country; paid offers are planned for launch.

Does Pro include the collections?

No. Collections and bundles are separate purchases and work with or without Pro. No subscription either way.

Can I see a collection before buying it?

Yes. In Finishes every set can be previewed — board, case and fittings — without changing your current game or finish.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Finishes and choose Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Every collection you own comes back.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
