Privacy Policy

Duo-Chess for iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

Duo-Chess collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and I run no server the app talks to. Your games and settings are stored only on your device.

The app requests no permissions — no camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone. The only outside services involved are Apple’s: the App Store for downloads and optional purchases, and Game Center if you choose to sign in.

Controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR for the processing described below. There is no statutory obligation to appoint a data protection officer.

2. What is stored on your device

The current game (position, move history, mode, clocks and any pending promotion), your training progress, local Game Center progress and your settings (finish, piece size, smooth piece moves, sounds and haptics, tabletop view) are saved in Duo-Chess’s own storage on your device.

None of this is transmitted to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes all of it. iOS may include this data in your own device backup, as with any app.

3. No tracking

Duo-Chess contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in iOS, Apple may provide me with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

4. App Store and in-app purchases

When you obtain Duo-Chess through the App Store, Apple processes the data needed for the download and your Apple Account as an independent controller. I receive only aggregated sales and download figures.

Duo-Chess Pro, the collector collections and the bundles are optional one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to load prices and to check the signed purchases on your device; I run no purchase server and receive no payment or account data.

If you test Duo-Chess through TestFlight, Apple shares with me the email address you were invited with and, if you choose to send them, crash reports, feedback and screenshots. This is governed by Apple’s TestFlight terms.

5. Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in through the board menu, Apple’s GameKit sends your achievements and your solo-win totals for the three leaderboards to Apple, linked to your Game Center profile. Apple processes this as an independent controller under its own privacy policy; other players may see your leaderboard entries depending on your Game Center settings. I receive no personal data from it.

Without signing in, progress stays on the device only. You can sign out at any time in Settings → Game Center.

6. Contacting me

If you email me, I process your email address, your message and anything you include (for example your device model) to answer it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to using the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Emails are deleted once the matter is resolved, unless statutory retention periods apply.

7. This website

This website is static. When you visit it, the hosting provider processes technical connection data — IP address, time of request, requested file, referrer and user agent — in server logs, which is necessary to deliver the page. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no external fonts or scripts. Your light/dark preference is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

8. Your rights

You have the right to information (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). One email to info@hildel.com is enough. Apart from emails, Duo-Chess stores no personal data on my side.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for me that is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

9. Changes

If Duo-Chess gains features that change how data is handled, this page will be updated before those features ship. The date at the top shows the current version.
